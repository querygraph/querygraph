The QueryGraph stack

Shared data, shared meaning, capable agents

Sail · LakeCat · Pinax · TypeSec · MCP · Marciana · Grust

Introduce the whole stack. A company has data in a lakehouse, a catalog of physical tables, a registry of common table shapes, and agents that discover and use them. No prior demo or product version knowledge is assumed.

A company asks a question

“Where is our customer data, and what can an analytics agent do with it?”

  • Business teams use different words for the same concept
  • Systems represent similar records with different columns
  • Agents need meaning, structure, and usable operations
Explain a large enterprise with many teams and datasets. Pinax helps standardize common table shapes and connects shared definitions to actual fields. We demonstrate the process on a small synthetic fixture, not a company-wide production inventory.

Console steps 3–4 · Discover tables, then consult the ontology

Pinax owns the central ontology. A name match returns a definition and a physical binding. Complete coverage refers to the registered inventory. New schema imports remain drafts until reviewed. The MCP action searches account number; the direct ontology action searches Customer identifier.

Agents with TypeSec access

An identified agent discovers the tables and fields appropriate to its task.

  • The analytics agent carries a TypeSec identity
  • Pinax returns its usable table inventory
  • Its requested operation carries a purpose and signature

Access is part of the normal workflow, alongside schema and meaning.

Introduce TypeSec affirmatively as what enables authenticated agents to work across the stack. The primary story is successful discovery and execution. Optional field and purpose checks are available after the main walkthrough. The public demo identity is only for synthetic fixture records.

Marciana and semantic workflows

QueryGraph composes semantic and cognition capabilities into agent workflows.

  • Navigator packages descriptions for discovery and reuse
  • Specialists contribute domain summaries
  • A supervisor coordinates and a synthesis agent combines
  • TypeSec identities and lineage accompany the work

Console steps 9–10 · Semantic bundle and specialist workflow

Navigator creates Croissant, CDIF, identity, and usage layers. QGLake Resilience Desk is deterministic specialist orchestration, not fresh model inference or a scan of every named dataset. Each action consults the central ontology before its fixture workflow. For the concrete ontology-derived read, use MCP step 5.
